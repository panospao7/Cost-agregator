## 1. Pipeline theme

Notification Capture: a `NotificationListenerService` gates, dedupes, filters and persists financial notifications into a durable encrypted intake queue, processed by a bounded-concurrency pipeline into expenses/review rows with a full diagnostic ledger. The dominant risks in this area are privacy (raw notification text, extras JSON, sensitive keys), cancellation loss on service shutdown, and filter over/under-capture. This session's fresh look confirms the 2026-06-01 fix marathon held up through the later time-migration, cancellation/atomicity (PR18–PR24) and PII burn-down commits — with one filter regression and a few small hygiene findings.
