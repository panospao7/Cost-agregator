## 1. Pipeline theme

Transaction Lifecycle: a single coordinator owns every expense mutation (create/update/delete/bulk) with write-barrier checks, snapshot-inside-transaction (TOCTOU-safe) reads, dedupe-key regeneration with in-transaction collision checks, durable `TransactionEvent` audit rows in the same transaction as the mutation, and post-commit side effects through a planner/runner with trigger-correct types and time-stamped idempotency keys. This session confirms the U-PR2 TOCTOU overhaul, the P2-PR1/PR2/PR3 fixes, and the U-PR7/U-PR8 contracts all held through the later time-migration (da2b8565..96c6b27d), cancellation/atomicity (PR18–PR24), and PII burn-down commits. Remaining findings are hygiene-level: inconsistent CE handling on best-effort event writes and `e.message` flowing into a durable audit `reason` field.
